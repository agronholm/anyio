Fixed reference cycles that kept the frames of a child task alive until the cyclic
garbage collector ran, when the task was cancelled on the asyncio backend (Python 3.11
and later), when it was started with ``TaskGroup.start()`` on the asyncio backend and
then raised an exception or returned before calling ``task_status.started()``, or when
it was started with ``TaskGroup.start()`` on the Trio backend and then raised an
exception or was cancelled by a cancel scope
